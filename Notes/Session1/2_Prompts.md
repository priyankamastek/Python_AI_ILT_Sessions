# What is Prompt Engineering?
Prompt engineering is the process of designing and refining text instructions — called prompts — given to Generative AI models to produce desired outputs

# Key Purpose:

To guide the AI's response so it generates the most accurate, relevant, and useful results for your specific needs

# Why It Matters:

- Clear and specific prompts directly influence the quality and accuracy of AI output.

 - It is a vital skill for anyone working with AI -  developers, data engineers and non-technical stakeholders.

"Think of prompt engineering as a conversation with a very capable new team member. The better you brief them, the more likely they will understand the task and deliver what you need."

# Prompt Engineering
1. Language use - Natural Language
2. Who can Do it? - Anyone
3. Instructions GO TO - AI language LLM models (Opus, Sonnet, gpt 5)
4. Output Control - Probabilistic — guided by prompt quality.
5. Iteration Style - 	Refine, re-prompt, verify
6. How You Check It - Run the generated code, run the tests, review the diff

LLM is the engine, Prompts are steer wheel (You steer the model by what you write)

The quality of your prompt directly determines the quality of the AI's output
